PAK GSM REPAIR UPDATE 3.3 - FUTURE APP UPDATE SYSTEM

1. Host the app at a public HTTPS URL.
2. The APK/TWA should point to that HTTPS PWA URL.
3. For a future web update (3.4, 3.5, etc.):
   - Replace the hosted index.html/cloud-config.js/other web assets with the new version.
   - Change the Service Worker CACHE_NAME in sw.js (example: pgr-3.4-shell-v1).
   - Update update.json latestVersion and notes.
   - Publish all files together.
4. Users can open Settings > App Update > Check for Update.
5. If a newer version is detected, Update Now refreshes the PWA and activates the new Service Worker.

IMPORTANT:
- Do NOT delete localStorage or IndexedDB as part of an update.
- Ordinary web-feature updates do not require rebuilding the APK.
- Changes to native Android/TWA settings require a new APK/AAB build.
- Keep the Android signing key safe; it is needed for future Android releases.
- The Apps Script Code.gs still needs its one-time deployment/update when you are ready for the 3.3 backend.
